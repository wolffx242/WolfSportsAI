WolfSportsAI V6.5 Turbo Autopilot

Performance architecture:
- NBA and NFL live odds/scores refresh in parallel.
- Background worker wakes every 60 seconds but only performs work when due.
- Live odds/results target refresh every 5 minutes.
- Completed-game count change detection.
- Retraining happens when new completed games are detected, when a model is missing,
  or as a 4-hour safety cadence.
- Historical bootstrap is retried at most once every 24 hours when missing.
- A retrain's chronological holdout evaluation is saved as the backtest result,
  avoiding duplicate model training.
- Full safety backtest remains every 12 hours.
- UI stays non-blocking and uses cached/local data from V6.4.
- Player/API caches from V6.4 retained.

Recommended production server:
2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM or better for the best cost/performance balance.
