WolfSportsAI V7 Matchups / Pick-First UI

Requested redesign:
- Matchup pages now use a compact table/card layout inspired by mainstream sportsbook analytics sites.
- WolfSportsAI branding and original styling retained; no third-party branding/assets copied.
- Main columns: Matchup, selected market/best price, AI Win %, AI Lean.
- AI Win % replaces public "% of bets" style information.
- AI Win % means the model's straight-up probability of each team winning the game.
- Moneyline is the default view because it directly answers which team is more likely to win.
- Users can switch Moneyline / Spread / Total / All.
- Spread view shows each team's current spread and price while AI Win % remains straight-up game win probability.
- Total view shows Over/Under line and price while AI Win % still shows team game-win probabilities.
- Clicking a matchup still opens the detailed Game Center.
- Existing Autopilot V6.5 performance architecture retained.
