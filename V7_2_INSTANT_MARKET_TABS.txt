WolfSportsAI V7.2 Instant Market Tabs

Critical speed changes:
- team_consensus + team model apply + enrichment is cached for 15 minutes per exact odds snapshot.
- Moneyline, Spread and Total sport slices are cached separately.
- NBA/NFL market selector now runs inside st.fragment.
- Clicking Moneyline / Spread / Total reruns only the market-board fragment, not the full app.
- All market data is precomputed from the same in-memory team dataframe.
- Manual live refresh invalidates relevant caches so new lines appear correctly.
- Opening a matchup forces a full app rerun to navigate into Game Center.
- V7.1 scheduled maintenance remains unchanged.

Expected behavior:
After the first NBA/NFL page load, market-tab switches should be near-instant because
they no longer rerun consensus/model enrichment or the rest of the Streamlit page.
